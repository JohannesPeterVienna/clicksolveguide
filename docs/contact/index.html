Corrections

If a menu path or command doesn't match what you see, tell us the page, the step, your system and its version (for example “Windows 11 24H2” or “macOS 15”) and what your screen shows instead. Confirmed errors are corrected and the page's review date changes.

No personal tech support

We don't offer individual troubleshooting, phone calls or remote sessions, and we never will. If someone contacts you claiming to be ClickSolve Guide support, it is not us. For your device, contact its maker through its official website; for your connection, use the contact details on your provider's contract or bill.
