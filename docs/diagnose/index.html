Guided diagnoses

Each diagnosis asks one question at a time. Every answer crosses layers off the map until the likely culprit is left – then you see only the steps that fit, for your system.

The diagnoses run entirely in your browser. Your answers and notes are kept in this browser's local storage so you can come back after restarting the router; nothing is sent to us or anyone else. At any point you can download the fix log as a text file or print it. It lists the checks you did, your answers, the result and the recommended steps with their menu paths – the kind of record that saves time with an internet provider's support line, a company help desk or a repair shop.

Every step carries a risk label. “Harmless” steps change nothing. “Changes a setting” comes with the undo. “Admin needed” means you will be asked for an administrator password. “Last resort” steps remove saved settings and only appear after the gentler checks. If a diagnosis reaches a security warning – for example a certificate warning on a website – it stops and tells you what is safe to do. It never explains how to click past such a warning.

Not sure which one to pick? If nothing loads at all, start with Wi-Fi connected but no internet. If only one address fails, start with one website won't load.
