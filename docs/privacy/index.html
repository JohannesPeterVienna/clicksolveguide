Privacy

Privacy policy

This policy describes which personal data is processed when you visit clicksolveguide.com, why, on what legal basis, and which rights you have.

Controller

AdStern, Seilerstätte 8, 1010 Vienna, Austria, email office@adstern.com. Further details are in the imprint.

Hosting

The site consists of static files served by GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. To deliver a page, your browser sends technical data to GitHub's servers: your IP address, the requested address, date and time, browser type and the referring page. GitHub uses this data to deliver and secure the service and may keep it in server logs for a limited time. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a reliable and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Transfers to the USA are based on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses. More in the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Diagnosis answers, notes and fix logs

The diagnoses run entirely in your browser. Your answers, the notes you type and the system version you choose are saved in your browser's local storage on your device so that a diagnosis can continue after a restart. The fix log is created in your browser when you download or print it. None of this is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising or analytics. You can delete it by clearing this site's data in your browser settings or with “Start over” in a diagnosis (which clears the answers; delete the notes text yourself).

Advertising with Google AdSense

Diagnosis and guide pages show advertising delivered by Google AdSense, provided by Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA). AdSense uses cookies and similar technologies to show ads, limit how often you see the same ad, measure performance and prevent fraud.

Third party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, at www.aboutads.info, or in Europe at www.youronlinechoices.eu. How Google uses data from sites that use its services is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent. If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent message from Google's certified consent management platform (IAB Transparency & Consent Framework) appears before cookies or similar technologies that require consent are used. Personalized ads require your consent. Non-personalized ads, which rely on page content and approximate location, also use cookies or identifiers for frequency capping, reporting and fraud prevention, so they also require consent to storing and accessing information on your device. Without that consent, Google can show only limited ads. Limited ads do not use cookies or local storage for personalised advertising; Google may still use them for fraud prevention and frequency limits as described in its documentation, and it processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the ads. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 165(3) of the Austrian Telecommunications Act 2021). You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with – the same link is in the footer of every page. If a U.S. state privacy law applies to your visit, the button opens Google’s U.S. opt-out dialog instead, where you can opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising; after an opt-out, ad personalization and Google Analytics stay off.

Google Analytics 4

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to learn which diagnoses and guides are used, so we can improve them. Before any Google script runs, every page sets Google Consent Mode to “denied” for advertising and analytics storage and switches Google Analytics off. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics is loaded only after you consent in the consent message to storing and accessing information on a device (TCF purpose 1), to Google as a vendor and to analytics. Elsewhere it is loaded only when the consent message reports analytics as granted or not requiring consent and confirms that you have not opted out under a U.S. state privacy law; missing or unclear signals keep it off. If you withdraw consent under “Privacy settings”, Consent Mode returns to “denied” and Google Analytics is switched off at once. Your diagnosis answers and notes are never sent to Google Analytics. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. Event data is deleted after the retention period set in our Google Analytics property. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Transfers to Google LLC in the USA are based on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Fonts and other resources

The fonts Schibsted Grotesk and JetBrains Mono are served from this website itself. Apart from the Google advertising and analytics scripts described above, the site loads no scripts, styles or fonts from other servers.

Email

If you write to us, we use your address and message only to answer and delete them when they are no longer needed for that, unless legal retention periods apply (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future. Because we do not store personal data about visitors ourselves, most requests concern Google; Google's own controls are at myaccount.google.com.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority – in Austria the Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna, dsb@dsb.gv.at, www.dsb.gv.at – or with the authority in the country where you live.

Notice for U.S. residents

Some U.S. state privacy laws, for example the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA, may treat advertising cookies used for cross-context behavioral advertising as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information. Where such laws apply, Google's consent platform offers a “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” choice on this site, which you can reopen at any time via “Privacy settings”. We do not sell personal information in any other way and do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16.
